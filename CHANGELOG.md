# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Boss stats are data. `data_maps/entity_type/base_attributes.json` sets the Greatwood Warden's and Exanimator's health, armour, damage and speed, and works for any living creature.
- Boss moves are data. `boss_moves.json` holds every damage, radius and cooldown of both bosses, phase two included.
- Thurible cloud distance and size are data (`data_maps/item/thurible.json`).
- Scythe enchantment numbers are data. `data_maps/enchantment/enchantment_values.json` holds Eclipse's damage, Soul Devourer's heal chances, Grim Harvest's chances, length and damage cut, and Reaper's Step's dash power, reach, damage and cooldown.
- docs/DATA.md lists everything a datapack can change.

## 0.8.0

- Gives the four scythes distinct moves with optional Fundamentum 1.27.2. Reaper's Step keeps priority on its dash input.
